# Update 13: Spaceport sales and belt spacing

## What changed

- Added a visible cargo dock and conveyor snap point at the starter spaceport.
- Conveyors can feed dust directly to the dock, or feed a dust press and carry its compacted output to the dock.
- The spaceport automatically sells delivered dust for 5 cr or compacted dust for 30 cr, less a 10% service fee.
- Presses now expose a separate compacted-product outlet.
- Conveyor items keep a minimum 0.65 m gap. Extractors pause when the belt entry is occupied, and items queue behind a blocked belt or machine instead of overlapping.
- Pressed items remain stored until the output belt has room.

## Controls

- Aim at the spaceport and press `E` to see the dock instructions and sale prices.
- Select a conveyor in the build menu, click its start point, then click the marked spaceport dock to finish the run.
